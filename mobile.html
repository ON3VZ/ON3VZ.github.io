VHF / UHF

Main radio, permanent in the car

Mounted once and left in place: not moved around, always ready when the engine starts.

Mobile transceiver

Yaesu FTM-510DE ASP

Dual-band for 2 m and 70 cm, 50 W, with APRS built in and the C4FM digital mode. Powered by its own LiFePO4 battery, so nothing is hardwired to the car battery.

  • Bands2 m and 70 cm
  • Power50 W
  • ExtraAPRS, C4FM
  • Power sourceLiFePO4 battery
ANTENNAS

On the roof

A magnet mount on the roof, with a full-size dual-band antenna or a compact one when discretion matters.

Primary antenna

Comet CA-2X4SR

Dual-band for 2 m and 70 cm, rated for 150 W. The main rooftop antenna on the Komunica magnet mount, and a solid performer.

2 m70 cm
Backup antenna

Comet SBB-2

Compact dual-band for 2 m and 70 cm, rated for 60 W. Used when the CA-2X4SR is not mounted, and ideal for discreet operation.

2 m70 cm
Magnet mount

Komunica BM-1 PL

Magnetic base with an SO-239 connector and 5 m of RG-58 cable. Holds the CA-2X4SR or the SBB-2 on the roof, no drilling required.

HANDHELD

Always in the pocket

Dual-band handheld

Yaesu FT-65

A 2 m and 70 cm VHF/UHF handheld, here on 144.675 MHz, the WLD club frequency. It is the backup for the FTM-510DE ASP in the car and the main radio for portable walk-around use.

With an SMA to PL-259 pigtail it connects straight to the Komunica magnet mount antenna: a full-size antenna with handheld convenience.
