Each country outline is split into its separate landmasses, and every landmass is coloured for the DXCC entity that occupies it. Two cases cannot be separated at this map resolution: England, Scotland and Wales share the single outline of Great Britain, and European and Asiatic Russia share one continuous landmass. Those shapes list every entity worked on them in the tooltip. A landmass belonging to a country you have worked but reserved for a different DXCC entity, such as the Canary Islands under Spain, stays dark until that entity itself is worked.
