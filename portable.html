Field Power Supply
LiFePO4 · 50Ah
A 50Ah lithium iron phosphate battery is the backbone of the portable station. LiFePO4 chemistry is the smart choice for portable radio: flat voltage discharge curve (stays near 13.2V until nearly empty), high cycle life (2000+ cycles), and safe chemistry — no thermal runaway. At 25W TX with typical WWFF duty cycles, a single charge supports a full day of field operation with plenty of margin.

QRP Portable Station · ON3VZ/P

5 Watts, on foot

A second, much smaller portable station built specifically for WWFF/ONFF activations: a QRP Labs QMX at 5 W into a SOTAbeams Band Hopper IV, run as an inverted V on a lightweight mast. Everything fits in a backpack, and it operates under a separate callsign, ON3VZ/P.

● 5 W QRP
Transceiver · HF QRP

QRP Labs QMX

A five-band QRP transceiver about the size of a pack of cards, 220 g including its enclosure, doing CW, digital modes and SSB at 5 W. A single USB-C cable carries both CAT control and audio, so the whole rig-to-laptop interface is one cable. Runs from a LiFePO4 pack regulated down to 11.50 V through an adjustable buck converter — the QMX's supply range is a strict 6.0–12.0 V.

A separate identity, on purpose. ON3VZ/P has its own QRZ.com logbook, LoTW certificate and Log4OM configuration, kept deliberately apart from the shack's ON3VZ log. Dual Band Handheld · VHF/UHF

Yaesu FT-65

A rugged 2m/70cm handheld — the portable VHF/UHF companion. Primary radio for POTA walk-around use on site, and a solid backup for the FTM-510DE ASP when not in the car. A key trick: via a SMA-to-PL259 pigtail it connects directly to the Komunica BM-1 magnet mount — giving the handheld the benefit of a full-size rooftop antenna with no extra hardware.
