Behind the mic

Kristof, ON3VZ

This is where it happens. Heil headset on, the IC-7300 MK II on the desk, AetherSDR on one screen and Log4OM on the laptop.

On FT8 the vertical usually reaches furthest. On SSB, stations regularly tell me I sound clean and loud, both with the stock hand microphone and with the Heil headset. That feedback made me curious about why, and it shaped how this station is built.

HF

HF station

One radio, two antennas: a vertical for DX on the higher bands and a wire for 80 and 40 m.

Transceiver

ICOM IC-7300 MK II

The heart of the HF station, used at 25 W, the Class C limit. Its built-in scope runs on the large screen next to it.

The transmit audio is tuned with the radio's own equalizer and a quiet ALC.

Antennas

DX antenna · 20, 15, 10 m

RF.Guru IronWave 6

A free-standing vertical of about 6 m in the middle of the garden. At the base sit a 4:1 unun from RF.Guru and a radial plate with 32 radials, now buried in the lawn. A line isolator follows right after the base.

It is the antenna for DX on 20, 15 and 10 m, and it works on 30 m with extra tuning.

Temporary · 80 and 40 m

HyEndFed 5 Band

A 23 m end-fed wire. The feed point sits about 9 m up on the flat roof, and the wire slopes down across the garden to a short pole.

It came from the portable kit to add 80 and 40 m at home. Once the permanent low-band antenna is ready, it goes back into the portable bag.

EMC

A clean signal on 25 W

There is no single trick. Every cable that enters the shack is a door for RF and noise, in both directions, so every door gets its own filter or choke. Together they keep my own signal in the antenna and the neighbourhood noise out of the receiver.

Door 1 · Coax

Chokes along every feed line

RF.Guru chokes at the antenna, along the line and before the radio, so the outside of the coax stays quiet.

Door 2 · Earthing

Antenna box and busbar

Both feed lines enter through one box on the outside wall. Inside the shack, everything is bonded to one busbar.

Door 3 · Mains

A filtered circuit of its own

The shack has its own sub panel with a Schaffner mains filter. A second filter sits on the solar inverter.

Door 4 · Equipment cables

Ferrites on HDMI, USB and audio

Every cable between devices can act as an antenna, so each one gets a ferrite.

Door 5 · Network

Shielded network cable

The network enters the shack on a shielded cable, one less path for noise from computers and routers.
